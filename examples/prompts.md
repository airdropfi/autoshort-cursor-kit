# Example prompts

Paste any of these into Cursor (Agent) after the AutoShort server shows a green dot in Customize, then MCPs. Each one stops for your review before anything costs credits.

1. `Use AutoShort to draft a 30-second faceless short about why the moon looks bigger near the horizon. Show me the script as a table first and don't render anything yet.`
2. `Check my AutoShort credit balance and tell me how much it would cost to render draft <draft id>. Don't render.`
3. `Plan a 5-episode faceless story series about a lighthouse keeper and a talking seagull. Save the season plan, show it to me, and wait for my yes before producing anything. Create the series paused.`
4. `List my latest AutoShort videos with status and links.`
5. `Look at what has worked on my AutoShort account so far and suggest three formats to try next. Read-only.`
6. `Revise scene 3 of my last AutoShort video so the narration is shorter. Create a new draft and show me the price before rendering.`
